## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Stop missing high-volatility windows. Market Session Watch puts every forex session countdown, economic event, and XAUUSD bias signal on one screen — with alarms that punch through Android Doze mode and survive reboots.

Built by traders, for traders.

🕐 **8 GLOBAL SESSION TIMERS — LIVE COUNTDOWN**
Track all major forex trading sessions in real time:
• Pre-Asian, Asian Open, Pre-London, London Open, London Close, Pre-NY, NY Open & NYSE
• Animated progress rings show exactly how far into each session you are
• Peak sessions highlighted so you focus on the highest-liquidity windows
• All times synced to UTC — no timezone confusion

⏰ **ALARMS THAT ACTUALLY FIRE**
Most notification tools fail when your phone sleeps. Not this one.
• Native Android alarms that wake your screen — even in Doze mode
• 15-minute interval reminders during active sessions keep you engaged
• Alarms survive device reboots — they auto-restore on power-up
• Auto-detects vibrate/silent mode and syncs notification behavior
• Choose your own ringtone for session alarms and reminders separately

📅 **EMBEDDED ECONOMIC CALENDAR**
High-impact economic events from the currencies that move gold and forex:
• Covers USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD & AUD events
• Powered by TradingView — reliable, up-to-date data
• Plan your entries around NFP, CPI, interest rate decisions & more
• No need to switch between tools — everything lives in one place

🥇 **AI-POWERED XAUUSD GOLD BIAS**
Get a daily fundamental read on Gold before you trade:
• AI analysis delivered via email, parsed and displayed automatically
• Clear bullish / bearish / neutral sentiment indicator
• Summary of key drivers — DXY, yields, geopolitics, Fed outlook
• Make informed decisions instead of guessing direction

🎨 **DESIGNED FOR NIGHT-OWL TRADERS**
• Full dark theme — easy on the eyes during late-night Asian session
• Clean, minimal interface with smooth animations (Framer Motion)
• Haptic feedback on key interactions
• Grouped session view: Asian, London & New York at a glance

🔋 **RELIABILITY FEATURES**
• Requests battery optimization exemption so alarms stay on time
• Exact alarm scheduling permission for precision timing
• Works offline once loaded — no constant internet dependency
• Lightweight and fast — no bloat, no ads
