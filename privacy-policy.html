Privacy Policy

Market Session Watch — by BZOE Creativies

Effective Date: April 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Market Session Watch ("the App"), developed by BZOE Creativies ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App. Market Session Watch is a forex trading companion that provides informational gold (XAUUSD) analysis data. The App does not execute trades, collect personal data, use analytics, or require account creation.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The App accesses the following data solely for its core functionality:

  • Email content (read-only): The App uses the Gmail API with read-only access to fetch specific automated XAUUSD gold analysis emails sent by noreply@x.ai. No other emails are read, accessed, or stored. The App does not read your personal correspondence, contacts, drafts, or any other mailbox data.
  • Cached analysis data: Parsed analysis results are stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage mechanism. This data never leaves your device except as described in the "Third-Party Services" section below.

We do not collect:

  • Names, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Financial or payment information

2. How We Use Information

The limited data accessed by the App is used exclusively to:

  • Retrieve automated XAUUSD analysis emails from your Gmail inbox
  • Send retrieved email content to our backend server for parsing and formatting
  • Display parsed analysis data within the App for your reference
  • Deliver notifications about trading session times and new analysis availability

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize any data accessed through the App. The data is used solely to provide the App's core informational features.

3. Data Storage

Analysis data retrieved and parsed by the App is cached in your device's localStorage. This data remains on your device and is not synced to any cloud service or external database. You can clear this cached data at any time by clearing the App's data through your device settings.

Our backend server processes email content transiently for parsing purposes. Parsed data is returned to the App and is not permanently stored on the server.

4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Gmail API: Used with read-only access to fetch specific automated analysis emails from your Gmail account. Gmail API usage complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We access only the minimum data necessary (specific emails from noreply@x.ai) and do not use Gmail data for advertising, market research, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.
  • Zapier: Acts as an intermediary webhook service to facilitate email fetching. Zapier processes data in transit according to its own Privacy Policy.
  • Railway: Our backend server is hosted on Railway (market-session-watch-production.up.railway.app). Email content is sent to this server for parsing. Railway's infrastructure is governed by its own Privacy Policy.
  • TradingView: The App may embed or reference TradingView charting widgets for market data visualization. TradingView operates under its own Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services. We are not responsible for their data practices.

5. Android Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions, each used solely for its stated purpose:

  • Internet: Required to communicate with Gmail API, Zapier webhooks, and our backend server.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you about trading session times and new analysis data.
  • Exact Alarms: Used to schedule precise notifications aligned with forex market session openings and closings.
  • Vibrate: Provides haptic feedback for notifications.
  • Wake Lock: Ensures scheduled tasks and notifications execute reliably when the device is idle.
  • Receive Boot Completed: Allows the App to reschedule alarms and notifications after a device restart.
  • Request Ignore Battery Optimizations: Ensures timely delivery of session notifications by preventing the system from delaying background tasks.
  • Full Screen Intent: Enables full-screen notification alerts for critical session events.

No permissions are used to collect, track, or transmit personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

Market Session Watch is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
